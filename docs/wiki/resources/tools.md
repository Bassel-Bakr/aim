Utilities that sit around a trainer rather than replacing one. Each entry links to the tool's own
site, which is where its current features and pricing live.

<div class="aim-links" markdown>

- [Evxl Benchmark Tracker](https://evxl.app) — pulls your scores from your trainer profile and ranks
  them against community [benchmark](../training/benchmarks.md) sheets. No account step: you enter a
  Steam ID and it reads from there. Supports KovaaK's and Aimbeast.[^REF-25]
- [kova](https://kova.page) — a public profile collecting your peripherals, in-game
  [settings](../getting-started/setup.md), recent KovaaK's personal bests and your aimgroup on one
  shareable page. Also ships browser tools.[^REF-30]
- [Raw Accel](https://github.com/RawAccelOfficial/rawaccel) — a free, open-source Windows driver
  that applies mouse acceleration and
  [sensitivity](../getting-started/sensitivity.md) curves you define, before any game sees the
  input.[^REF-62][^REF-63]
- [KovOBS](https://github.com/Bassel-Bakr/KovOBS) — watches your KovaaK's stats folder and tells OBS
  to save a replay clip whenever you set a personal best. Written by this wiki's
  maintainer.[^REF-35]

</div>
